What we treat most often

The conditions patients ask about most

Each diagram shows exactly where the problem sits and how far the joint can safely move, the same way it's explained to you in your first home visit.

01 / Shoulder

Frozen Shoulder (Adhesive Capsulitis)

The shoulder capsule tightens and thickens, sharply limiting how far the arm can lift or rotate, often worse at night.

Restricted lift Night pain Stiff capsule
02 / Lower Back

Lower Back Pain & Sciatica

Pressure on the lumbar spine irritates the sciatic nerve, sending pain, tingling or numbness down the back of the leg.

Nerve pain Stiff mornings Leg numbness
03 / Knee

Knee Osteoarthritis

Cartilage wear at the knee joint causes stiffness, swelling and a noticeably reduced ability to bend the knee fully.

Joint stiffness Swelling Reduced bending
04 / Neck

Cervical Pain (Neck Stiffness)

Muscle tightness and joint stiffness in the cervical spine limit how far the head can turn, often radiating pain into the shoulder.

Restricted turn Muscle spasm Referred pain
